## Tips for every future episode

- Ask the AI to return **one source per claim** while writing the script. Reviewing is much faster.
- Watch out for: exact years, "first ever", quotes attributed to famous people, death tolls, money conversions.
- If unsure, hedge it in the script ("the story goes…", "around…", "historians aren't sure…"). That also sounds more trustworthy.
