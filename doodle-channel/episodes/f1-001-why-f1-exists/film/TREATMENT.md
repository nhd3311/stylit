# Treatment: Why does F1 even exist?

**Logline.** F1 wasn't invented in one go: it is the answer to four problems people hit one after another between 1894 and 1950.

**Candidate structures**
1. *Chronological* 1894 → 1950, plain history. Safe, but the "why" gets lost.
2. *Four problems* (prove it works → a safe place → make it fair → one champion), each closing with a checklist tick. **Chosen**: the title asks "why", so the structure answers it, and the checklist gives a recurring beat viewers can follow.
3. *Object story*: follow the Alfa Romeo 158 only. Great hook, too thin for 8 minutes; kept as the payoff instead (hidden in the war → wins race 1).

**Shape.** Cold open at Silverstone 1950 (the answer first) → rewind with a rolling year counter → 4 problems → back to Silverstone, the hidden car wins → the four ticks → today.

**Look.** Risograph-editorial: flat inks with a fixed colour-plate offset (misregistration), warm paper, ink outlines; host Apex (white round head, goggles, orange gloves) acts in every era. National racing colours carry meaning: blue France, white Germany, red Italy, green Britain, silver for the Arrows.

**Sound.** Synthesised lo-fi band (e-piano, bass, brushed kit); mood per scene: light for the newspaper era, tense/minor for the race of death and the 1930s, pads only for the war, triumph at Silverstone, warm for the outro. Foley on cuts, stamps, ticks, passes and crowds. Music ducks under the voice.
